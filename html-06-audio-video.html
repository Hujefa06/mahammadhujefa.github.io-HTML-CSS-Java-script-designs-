HTML-06 Multimedia & Iframes

HTML5 Native Audio and Video Embedding

Demonstrates the inclusion of multimedia streams into web pages using the semantic <audio> and <video> elements with playback controls, multiple source formats, poster preview frames, loop, and muted attributes.

🎯 Practical Laboratory Objective
Understand browser multimedia codec support (MP4/WebM, MP3/OGG), attributes for media control (controls, muted, poster), and graceful fallback handling for older browsers.
